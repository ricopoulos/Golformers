## 📱 MARKETING STRATEGY (Top 3 Priorities)

### **1. Character-Driven Content (Not Product-Driven)**

Gen Z/Millennials buy into CHARACTERS first, products second.

**Do This:**
- "Meet Kai, the Puttmaster" (character intro videos)
- "Which Crestline Guardian are you?" (personality quiz)
- Character backstories on social media
- AR experiences featuring characters

**Not This:**
- "Buy our golf alignment cards"
- Feature-focused product descriptions

### **2. Community Over Customers**

Build belonging, not a buyer base.

**Do This:**
- "Crestline Seekers" or "Line Energy Collective" (community name)
- Discord/Reddit community before launch
- User-generated content challenges
- Collectible trading/showcasing platform

**Not This:**
- Email blast: "Buy now!"
- Transactional relationship

### **3. Scarcity + Social Proof**

**Do This:**
- "Genesis Set - First Edition (Numbered 1-1000)"
- Influencer early access program
- Limited drops (not always available)
- Show real collectors on social media

**Not This:**
- Mass production from day 1
- "Always in stock"

### **Phase 2: Soft Launch (4 weeks)**

**Goal:** Early access + social proof

**Tactics:**
1. Email list gets 48-hour early access

4. User-generated content campaign (#MyCrestline)
5. First tournament/challenge with Line Energy points

**KPI:** Sell out Genesis Set, 50+ UGC posts

### **Phase 3: Public Launch**

**Goal:** Mass awareness + FOMO

**Tactics:**
1. Public announcement (Genesis Set sold out, waitlist open)
2. Next drop date announced (scarcity maintained)
3. Mainstream golf media coverage
4. Retail partnerships explored
5. Crestline League beta (competitive community)

**KPI:** 10K+ waitlist, media coverage in 3+ golf publications
