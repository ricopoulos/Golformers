## 🚫 COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

### ❌ DON'T: Wait for perfection
- **DO:** Ship prototypes fast, iterate based on feedback
- First version will have flaws - that's okay
- Better to test imperfect product than perfect idea

### ❌ DON'T: Skip parent validation
- **DO:** Show mockups to 10+ parents before manufacturing
- Your team's opinion ≠ customer opinion
- One round of feedback saves thousands in wasted inventory

### ❌ DON'T: Ignore the kids
- **DO:** Test with actual 10-17 year olds
- Ask: "Would you want to collect these?" (brutally honest feedback)
- If kids say it's "lame," parents won't buy

### ❌ DON'T: Overproduce at first
- **DO:** Start with 5K units max
- Test product-market fit before scaling
- Easy to order more, hard to unload excess inventory

### ❌ DON'T: Launch without email list
- **DO:** Build waitlist for 4-6 weeks pre-launch
- Goal: 500+ emails before taking first order
- These are your first customers + word-of-mouth evangelists

**Options:**

### Decision 4: B2B vs B2C Focus

**Options:**
- **A)** B2C first (build brand, then approach tournaments)
- **B)** B2B first (wholesale revenue, then consumer marketing)
- **C)** Simultaneous (split focus 50/50)

**Recommendation:** C - Do both, but different intensity
- **Month 1-2:** 80% B2C (beta testing) + 20% B2B (pilot tournaments)
- **Month 3-6:** 50% B2C (scale marketing) + 50% B2B (sign partners)
- **Month 7-12:** 40% B2C (maintain) + 60% B2B (scale partnerships)
