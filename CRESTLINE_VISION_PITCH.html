It's not just a card. It's three things at once:

Your parents will buy it because it makes you better.

2. A Collectible Character

Each card features a legendary golfer—The Crestline Guardians—powered by Line Energy, a mysterious force that flows through every golf course on Earth.

🌙 LUNA — The Wind Whisperer

3. A Portal to a Digital Universe

Scan the QR code. Meet your Guardian. Watch their origin story. Unlock training challenges. See them come to life in AR on the course.

The card is just the beginning. The Line Energy universe is where the magic happens.

🚀 How It Starts

Phase 1: The Genesis Set

Four Founding Guardians. Limited first edition.

We're launching with Kai, Blaze, Luna, and Torque. Each card is numbered. Each represents a core golf skill. Together, they form the foundation of the Crestline universe.

  • Starter Set: All 4 Guardians in premium packaging
  • Single Packs: Random Guardian (collectible mystery)
  • Special Edition: First Edition numbered cards (for collectors)

Phase 2: The Expansion

New Guardians emerge. The universe grows.

Once kids collect the Founding Four, we introduce:

  • The Shadow Crestline (short game specialists)
  • The Storm Crestline (all-weather players)
  • The Ancient Crestline (legendary masters from golf history reimagined)

New characters. New powers. New stories. New reasons to collect.

This isn't a one-and-done product. It's a universe that evolves.

Phase 3: The Community

Crestline isn't just cards. It's a culture.

We're building:

  • Crestline League Tournaments (kids compete using their Guardians)
  • Trading Events (meet other collectors, trade, build sets)
  • Content Series (YouTube animated shorts, character backstories)
  • Mobile App (challenges, leaderboards, community)
  • Merch & Apparel (wear your Guardian's colors)

The goal: Make junior golf culture as rich and engaging as any gaming or anime fandom.
